## 1. Privacy policy

**Title:** Your data is used to reply to you. Nothing else.
**Intro:** What information I receive when you write to me, what I use it for and how you can ask me to delete it.
**Last updated:** [PUBLICATION DATE]

### Who is responsible
Mario Morera, an independent professional who runs this site (mmorera.agency). Contact for anything related to your data: mario@mmorera.agency.

### What data I receive
- **What you write in the project form:** name, email, company or project (optional), project stage, team context, description and estimated start.
- **What you send me by email or WhatsApp,** when you choose those channels.
- **Minimal technical data** any website receives in order to work (IP address, browser type), processed by the hosting provider.
Don't send passwords, credentials, payment details or sensitive information (such as health data) through these channels.

### What I use it for
- To read your enquiry, assess whether I can help and reply.
- To continue the conversation and, if we work together, prepare a proposal.
I don't sell your data, I don't use it for third-party advertising and I don't add you to mailing lists without your permission.

### Who it's shared with
Only with the services that keep the site and the communication running:
- **Site hosting:** Vercel.
- **Internal form notifications:** email and Telegram, so I see your enquiry in time.
- **Follow-up record (CRM),** when configured, so nothing gets lost.
Each provider handles data under its own policies and only to deliver that service.

### How long I keep it
While the conversation is active and up to 24 months after the last contact, unless you ask me to delete it sooner or a legal obligation requires keeping it longer.

### Your rights
You can ask me to **access**, **correct** or **delete** your data, or **object** to its use, by writing to mario@mmorera.agency. I reply within the time limits of the applicable law ([JURISDICTION]).

### Cookies
See the [cookie policy](/en/cookies).

### Minors
This site is meant for businesses and professionals. I don't knowingly receive data from minors.

### Changes
If this policy changes, I update the date above. Important changes are announced on this page.

## 3. Cookie policy

**Title:** Cookies: only what's needed.
**Intro:** What this site stores in your browser, and why.
**Last updated:** [PUBLICATION DATE]

### What I use today
This site **doesn't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.** It only stores preferences you choose, such as light or dark theme or language, so they're remembered on your next visit. That works without identifying you.

### If that changes
If I ever add analytics or any tool that uses non-essential cookies, I'll ask for your permission before turning it on and list it here, with its provider and purpose.

### How to delete them
You can delete what the site stores from your browser settings, under "Site data" or "Cookies".
